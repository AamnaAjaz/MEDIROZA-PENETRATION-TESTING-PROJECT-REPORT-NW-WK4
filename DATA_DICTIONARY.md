# Data dictionary

## staff_salary_records.csv
- `record_id`: row identifier from the dump.
- `employee_name`: employee name as transcribed.
- `job_title`: recorded role.
- `department`: recorded department.
- `monthly_salary_zar`: monthly salary in South African rand (ZAR).
- `date_joined`: date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- `transcription_confidence`: confidence in reading the photographed row; verify medium-confidence rows against the original SQL file.

## shareholder_records.csv
- `record_id`: row identifier from the dump.
- `shareholder_name`: shareholder name.
- `share_percent`: ownership percentage.
- `shares_held`: number of shares held.
- `share_class`: ordinary or preferential.
